Driving Formations

How We Line Up

Four formations. You'll be told which one over the radio before each segment. Just find your number and hold your spot.

Formation 1

Straight Line

Single file. Keep 2–3 car lengths between you. Match the lead car's speed. Used on bridges and single-lane roads.

  Direction →

  [911] ........... [1] — [2] — [3] — [4] — [5] — [6] — [7] — [8] ........... [RR]

  911 = Porsche pre-runner  |  RR = Range Rover tail
Bridges 25 MPH
Formation 2

Flying V

Arrowhead across 3 lanes. Car #1 leads center, others fan out. Requires a wide, clear road. The drone shoots this from above.

For Drivers

What to Know

Nothing complicated. Just a few things so everything runs smooth.

Basics

  • You'll get a number (1–8). That's your position in the convoy. Same spot every time.
  • Match the lead car's speed. Smooth and steady. The footage looks better when everyone's in sync.
  • Stay with the group. We're getting specific shots at each stop — save solo drives for after.
  • If anything comes up, keep going. The Range Rover support car handles any issues.

Radio Calls

Each car gets a walkie. Here's what you'll hear and what it means:

"LET'S GO" We're moving. Follow the car in front of you.
"HOLD UP" Stay put. Hold your speed.
"SHOOTING" Drone's up. Hold your line and speed.
"GOT IT" Shot's done. Relax.
"REGROUP" Formation broke up. Spread out and we'll reset at the next stop.
"PULL OVER" Something came up. Find a safe spot and stop.
🔄

If a formation doesn't come together: No worries. Spread out, keep cruising, and we'll set it up again at the next clear stretch. Nobody's keeping score.
